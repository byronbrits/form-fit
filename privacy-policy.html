Form Fit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Form Fit ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Form Fit application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Form Fit, we may collect information including:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Account and login information.
  • Fitness goals and personal fitness information.
  • Workout and exercise information.
  • Meal plans and nutrition-related information.
  • Progress information, measurements, and other information you choose to provide.
  • Progress photographs that you upload or take through the app.
  • Information that you share with your personal trainer through the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Form Fit account.
  • Provide fitness programs and meal plans.
  • Allow personal trainers to monitor and manage their clients' progress.
  • Allow you to submit progress photographs and fitness information.
  • Provide and improve the Form Fit service.
  • Communicate with you about your account or the service.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the application.

3. Information Shared With Personal Trainers

Form Fit is designed to allow personal trainers to manage their clients' fitness programs and monitor progress. Information that you choose to provide through the app, including fitness information, workout data, meal plans, and progress photographs, may be accessible to your personal trainer or the trainer associated with your account.

4. Progress Photographs

Form Fit allows users to take or upload photographs to track fitness progress. These photographs are treated as personal information and are used to provide the progress-tracking functionality of the app.

You should only upload photographs that you are comfortable storing and sharing through the Form Fit service and with your personal trainer.

5. Health and Fitness Information

Form Fit may process information relating to your fitness, exercise, nutrition, body measurements, goals, and progress. You provide this information voluntarily to use the features of the application.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

Form Fit may rely on third-party service providers to provide hosting, authentication, database, storage, analytics, payment, or other technical services required to operate the application.

These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle information in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Form Fit service, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

9. Deleting Your Information

If you would like to request deletion of your Form Fit account or personal information, please contact us using the email address below.

We will consider and process deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws and our legitimate business and legal requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Form Fit is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
